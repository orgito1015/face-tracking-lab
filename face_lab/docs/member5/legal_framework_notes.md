# Legal Framework Notes

## United States (BIPA)

- Default model in US: generally permitted unless state law restricts.
- BIPA regulates biometric identifiers in Illinois.
- Civil penalties may reach up to $5,000 per violation.

## European Union (GDPR + AI Act)

- Biometric processing is generally prohibited unless justified under strict legal grounds.
- GDPR Article 9 governs special category data.
- AI Act includes strict limits and bans for certain real-time remote identification uses.
- Maximum penalties can reach 20M EUR or 4% global annual turnover.

## Albania (Law No. 124/2024)

- GDPR-aligned framework; biometric processing is restricted unless justified.
- Enforcement by the national data protection authority (IDPC).
- Maximum penalties listed as ALL 2B or 4% turnover (subject to legal interpretation).
